# Delete your Ripped account

You can delete your account and all data stored on our servers at any time.

## In the app (fastest)

- Open Ripped and go to the **You** tab.
- Under **Data & privacy**, tap **Delete account**, then **Delete forever**.

This immediately deletes your account, every workout, set, plan, record and XP entry backed up to our servers, and clears the app on that phone.

## Without the app

Email ornobaadi.dev@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Ripped account". We'll delete your account and all associated data within 30 days and confirm by email.

## What is deleted

- Your account (Google email, name and profile picture)
- All backed-up training data: profile answers, plans, workouts, sets, progression, personal records and XP

We don't keep backups of deleted accounts. Crash reports never contain your personal data, so there's nothing to delete there.
