Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Ripped is a workout app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Short version: your training data lives on your phone, an account is optional, and we never sell or share your data for advertising.

What stays on your phone

Your answers from setup (goal, experience, equipment, schedule, joints to go easy on, units), your training plan, every workout and set you log, progression, records and XP are stored on your device. Without an account, none of this leaves your phone.

If you sign in

Signing in with Google is optional. If you do:

  • We receive your Google account's email address, name and profile picture so we can show who's signed in.
  • Your training data from the list above is backed up to our database so it's safe if you change phones, and kept in sync between your devices.
  • Your data is protected so that only your account can read or change it.

Our backend is provided by Supabase, which stores the data on our behalf.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no selling of data, no sharing with data brokers.
  • No location, contacts, photos or microphone access.
  • We don't read health data from other apps.

Crash reports

If the app crashes we may receive a technical crash report (device model, OS version, app version, the error). Crash reports never include your email, name or training values.

Notifications

Workout reminders are scheduled on your phone. They're off unless you turn them on.

Your choices

  • Export: You → Export my data gives you a full copy (JSON + CSV) at any time.
  • Delete: You → Delete account permanently removes your account and all backed-up data from our servers, and clears the app on that phone.
  • Without an account: uninstalling the app removes everything.

Children

Ripped isn't intended for children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll tell you in the app.
